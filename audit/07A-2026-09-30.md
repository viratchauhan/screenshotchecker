## Reproduction and changed behavior

Running the baseline dedicated extractor returned `₹500.00` for `₹500.00`, but `Not detected` for both `Amount: 500.00` and `Payment successful\n500.00`.

Both extractors now share a conservative fallback: an explicit amount/total field, or one standalone decimal in payment-status context. It preserves visible digits, does not manufacture a currency, and declines competing values. Dedicated fallback fact confidence is reduced relative to explicit currency; these scores are heuristic, not calibrated accuracy. General report marks inferred currency `Unknown`.

Labelled UTR/RRN/reference spans are excluded from phone matching in entity extraction and privacy scanning. References remain available as transaction entities and redactable reference findings. Digit-boundary guards prevent partial phone extraction from longer numbers.

Acceptance fixtures: 24 synthetic amount cases (currency symbol, lost symbol, labels, decimal/grouping, pending/failed text, ambiguous and negative controls), 14 labelled-reference cases (including digit bounding-box coverage), 2 long-reference cases, and 3 phone controls (US/UK/India). These are authored text fixtures version 1, not image OCR or a representative accuracy benchmark. They assert extraction only; no payment settlement/authenticity conclusion is permitted. No holdout image set exists yet.
